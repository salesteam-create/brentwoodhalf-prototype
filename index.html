/ The Story

Brentwood's own, since 1982.

It began as a Rotary Club of Brentwood à Becket fundraiser — a 9-mile run that grew, by 1984, into the half marathon the town now plans its spring around. Four decades on, it's the largest race of its kind in Essex, raising an estimated £3.8 million for good causes along the way.

For 2026 it steps up again: Barry Hearn OBE and Matchroom have committed a landmark five-year, £100,000+ title sponsorship — bringing fight-night production to one of the friendliest start lines in the country, on the streets where it all started.
