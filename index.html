/ The Story

Four decades. One iconic Essex morning.

Since 1982, the Brentwood Half has grown from a local club run into the largest race of its kind in Essex — a fixture in the spring running calendar that thousands chase a PB at every year.

For 2026 the race steps up again: Barry Hearn OBE and Matchroom have committed a landmark five-year, £100,000+ title sponsorship — bringing fight-night production values to one of the friendliest start lines in the country.
